🏭 Training Dashboard

Monitor ground-truth annotation coverage, export training data, and create custom AI models from your corrections.

How does training work?
  1. Correct extracted fields on the Records page.
  2. Click Mark All as Ground Truth — this saves your verified values as training data in the corrections table.
  3. Return here and click Export Training Data to download a ZIP of annotated images and labels.
  4. Use Apply to AI Model to build a custom Ollama model from your corrections — no GPU or weight changes required.
  5. Switch the active model in the Model Management section below.

The more records you annotate, the more accurate future extractions will be. Aim for coverage of all 14 NMVITIS fields across multiple states.
